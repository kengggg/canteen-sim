# Canteen Sim — Plan 5: Model v2 (Plates Stay, People Leave Before Food) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or
> superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the walk-away-with-takeaway rule with model v2: no takeaway, groups split after circling, and
groups leave before food (at the door, or from a queue). Update metrics, consumers, stored results and docs to match.

**Architecture:**
- Engine behaviour stays in `src/sim/agents.ts`, on top of `World` state (`world.ts`), `Stalls` (`stalls.ts`) and
  the pure helpers in `search.ts` and `seating.ts`.
- A new *party* layer lets groups shrink (members leave) and split (a remainder becomes its own party). Each person
  maps to a party, and each party points back to its original group.
- Metrics stay RunMetrics (one run, hashed) and PairMetrics.
- Consumers (batch catalogue, CSV, UI, Findings) follow the renamed metrics.

**Tech Stack:** Node (22 pinned; 26 works locally), TypeScript strict, Vitest, fast-check, Preact, three.js,
Playwright, ESLint allowlist on `src/sim/**`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-model-v2-plates-design.md` (called *design* below). It amends
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-24-canteen-sim-design.md` (called *main spec* below).

**Plan form (ruling, as Plans 2–4).** The same agent writes and executes this plan in one session, with both specs
loaded.
- Each task lists its files, exact interfaces and every test with its assertion.
- Implementation code is written during the task under TDD, not duplicated here.
- The final whole-branch review is the independent gate.

## Global Constraints

**Engine rules**
- **Integer units:** ms and mm. Drawn durations are `max(1, Math.round(s·1000))`; setting durations are
  `Math.round(s·1000)`.
- **Allowlist** on `src/sim/**` (no `**`, only the listed `Math.*`). Every sort comparator is total with an id
  tie-break.
- **New stream** `leave = 12`, key `groupId`. `L_g = lognormalMs(leave.waitMean, leave.waitCV, u)`.
  `R_g = leave.roomNeeded === 0 ? 0 : max(1, Math.round(leave.roomNeeded · Math.round(waitMean·1000) / L_g))`.

**Settings and defaults**

| Setting | Default | Range | Step |
|---|---|---|---|
| `leave.waitMean` | 600 s | 30–3600 s | 30 s |
| `leave.waitCV` | 0.5 | 0–1.5 | 0.05 |
| `leave.roomNeeded` | 3 | 0–20 | 1 |
| `search.splitAfter` | 120 s | 0–1800 s | 30 s |

- `search.patience` is removed.
- **`MODEL_VERSION = 2`.**

**Primary endpoints (frozen)**
- **P1:** left without eating % = (door + queue leavers) ÷ arrivals × 100.
- **P2:** time carrying a plate = mean over served people of (sit start − service end), in minutes.
- **P3 and P4:** unchanged.

**Event order** (design §2.7)
- A kind-4 service start at position 1 beats a deferred leave.
- The queue-leave timer and the split timer are kind 7.
- The pick-up end is kind 1. The walk back to the walkway stop ends in kind 3.

**Protocol** (design §5)
- **No A-vs-B outcome numbers** of model 2 may be computed, printed or read until the B-only check (Task 7) passes.
  That covers precompute, findings, batch runs, the mechanism test and printing A-vs-B metrics.
- **Still allowed:** rule tests, invariants, per-canteen sanity checks, hashes, event counts and timings.
- **B-only check** (defaults, seeds 1–30, 0%): mean P1 ≤ 20%, mean P2 ≤ 10 min, no truncation. If it fails, stop and
  report the B-only figures to the owner.
- **Never change rules or defaults to pass a test.** Report instead.

**Budgets**
- The bench median is ≤ 3 s per lunch (A at 100% and B).
- The default 150-run sweep takes ≤ 3 min with ≥ 4 workers.

## Review Focus

These are inputs the design implies but no task's tests exercise directly. Each line's test is added to the task named.

1. **`leave.roomNeeded` above the number of tables that could ever fit** (for example 20 on a 1 × 3 layout). Almost
   everyone leaves at the door once a seat is taken. The run must still terminate with exited = arrivals.
   *Test in Task 5:* `roomNeeded 20 on a 3-table layout terminates and every door leaver exits`.
2. **Splitting with `search.parallel` on and pending asks when the remainder forms.** Asks and searchers must move to
   the remainder party without being counted twice. *Test in Task 3:* `parallel split: remainder keeps its searchers,
   memory and pending asks`.
3. **A reserving group's last member giving up while waiting because every queue is full** (it is not in a slot). It
   must still walk to the claim node, pick up the object and exit. *Test in Task 5:* `last reserver leaving from an
   all-full wait still collects the object`.
4. **A queue-leave timer that falls on the ms a service starts.** Kind 4 comes first, so the person is served and
   stays. *Test in Task 4:* `requestLeave after service start returns 'served'`. *Test in Task 5:* `no leaver was ever
   served`.
5. **Model-1 links, settings codes and JSON that still carry `search.patience`.** They load with the model-version
   notice, patience is ignored, and the new settings take their defaults. *Test in Task 6:* `a model-1 link with
   patience loads with the notice and default leave settings`.

### Task 4: Leaving a queue, in Stalls (design §2.3)

**Interfaces (produces)**
- `Stalls.requestLeave(p): 0 | 1 | 2`:
  - `0 LEAVE_NOW` when the person is IDLE in a slot. It is removed now: `members[s]` is spliced, `queueLength[s]--`,
    and the waiting and in-slot counts drop if it had joined.
  - `1 DEFERRED` when it is walking in or moving up: `leavePending[p] = 1`.
  - `2 SERVED` when its service has started.
- In `settle()`: if `leavePending[p]` and not (`physPos === 1` and logical 1), clear the flag, remove the person and
  call `host.onDeferredLeave(p)`. If `physPos === 1` and logical 1, serve it and clear the flag.
- After any removal, every IDLE member behind with `physPos > logical` starts a move-up.
- `Stalls.releaseChoice(p)`: `queueLength[chosen[p]]--; chosen[p] = −1`. It is used when a leaver has chosen a stall
  but not reached it.
- `Stalls.walkBack(p): number`:
  - sets the path current slot → corner → walkway stop at walk speed, where the corner is
    `(top ? stopX : slot.x, top ? slot.y : stopY)`;
  - leg times use `max(1, ceilDiv(mm·1000, walk))`;
  - returns the arrival ms.
- `Stalls.leaveMs: Float64Array`.
- `StallHost.onDeferredLeave(p: number): void`.

**Interfaces**
- **Produces, in `agents.ts`:**
  - `export function doorVerdict(a: { minQueueMs: number; waitLimitMs: number; fitTables: number; roomNeeded: number }): 0 | 1 | 2 | 3`.
    It is pure:
    - `1` means the queue check failed (`minQueueMs > waitLimitMs`);
    - `2` means the seating check failed (`roomNeeded > 0 && fitTables < roomNeeded`);
    - `3` means both failed;
    - `0` means the group stays.
  - `export function fitTables(occMask: Int32Array, claimedBy: Int32Array, k2: number, n: number): number`. It counts
    unclaimed tables with `k2 − popcount(occ) ≥ n`. This is a pure O(tables) scan, used by tests and the invariant
    only.
  - `World.fitCount: Int32Array(k2 + 1)`: unclaimed tables by their number of unoccupied seats. `recomputeTable`,
    claim and unclaim keep it up to date. The door check reads `Σ_{c ≥ n} fitCount[c]` in O(k2), which respects main
    §12.2 (no per-event work proportional to tables). The invariant checks it against `fitTables`.
  - View gains `leftKind: Uint8Array` (per person) and `splitting: Uint8Array` (the person's party is in split mode),
    for the hover-card states.

**Rulings for this task**
- **Timer start.** The queue timer is scheduled when `queueStartMs[p]` is first set. That happens on reaching a
  walkway stop, for `PU.STALL` or `PU.FULLSTOP`.
- **Timer handling by phase:**
  - `SERVING`, or `serviceStartMs ≥ 0`: nothing;
  - `QUEUE`: `requestLeave`;
  - `FULL_WAIT`: leave now from the walkway node;
  - `TO_STALL`: `releaseChoice`, then re-plan to the exit (main §4.2 rule 8);
  - `TO_FULLSTOP`: re-plan to the exit.
- **Holding-seat party after a leave.** If a party with seated members reaches `eatDone === size`, schedule the stand
  start at `max(now, G.maxEatEndMs + lingerMs)`.
- **Claimed table after a leave.** If `sitStarted === size > 0`, the table becomes `complete`. With `size === 0` it
  stays `claimedEmpty` until the pick-up ends.
- **Leaver's seat.** A seat held for the leaver is released; `seatGroup` and `seatPerson` are reset and the table is
  recomputed.
- **Searcher re-target.** When a member leaves an uncommitted free party, each of its searchers that stands at a node
  (not on an edge, not asking) re-targets at once with `searchStep` for the smaller size. The others re-target at
  their next node, or decide at their ask's end with the current size.
- **Reserving party still searching.** If a member leaves a party whose claim search is under way, its `sumCache` is
  cleared.
